High-energy astrophysics

X-ray pulsars & magnetized neutron stars

I combine broadband X-ray spectroscopy, timing and polarimetry. Highlights include the first X-ray polarization detection from a long-period pulsar and the discovery of frequency hysteresis of quasi-periodic oscillations in an X-ray pulsar.
