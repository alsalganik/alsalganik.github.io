Detection and luminosity-dependent evolution of the high-energy hump in the Be/X-ray pulsar 1A 1118−61

Dense SRG/ART-XC and NuSTAR monitoring of the 2026 outburst traces how the spectrum of 1A 1118−61 develops a distinct high-energy hump as the source fades, constraining the transition to L ≈ (0.8–1.8)×10^36 erg/s.

Citation

Alexander Salganik, S. S. Tsygankov, S. V. Molkov, H. Xiao, Q. C. Zhao, L. Ji, A. A. Mushtukov, I. Yu. Lapshov, A. A. Lutovinov, A. Yu. Tkachenko, H. Feng, S. N. Zhang, X. B. Li, S. Zhang, J. Poutanen

A&A, in press (2026).

View arXiv record

Summary

Dense SRG/ART-XC and NuSTAR monitoring of the 2026 outburst traces how the spectrum of 1A 1118−61 develops a distinct high-energy hump as the source fades, constraining the transition to L ≈ (0.8–1.8)×10^36 erg/s.

  • Two-hump spectrum with maxima near 10 keV and 30–40 keV at low luminosity
  • Cyclotron line at ~55 keV with no significant luminosity dependence
