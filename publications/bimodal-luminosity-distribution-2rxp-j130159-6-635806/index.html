Discovery of a bimodal luminosity distribution in persistent Be/X-ray pulsar 2RXP J130159.6-635806

This paper identifies a bimodal luminosity distribution in the persistent Be/X-ray pulsar 2RXP J130159.6-635806: after reaching spin equilibrium, the source revealed a new stable low-luminosity accretion state about ten times fainter than before.

Citation

Alexander Salganik, S. S. Tsygankov, M. Chernyakova, D. Malyshev, J. Poutanen

Astronomy & Astrophysics 698, A71 (2025).

View DOI record

Summary

This paper identifies a bimodal luminosity distribution in the persistent Be/X-ray pulsar 2RXP J130159.6-635806: after reaching spin equilibrium, the source revealed a new stable low-luminosity accretion state about ten times fainter than before.

  • Documents bimodal luminosity behavior in a persistent Be/X-ray pulsar
  • Builds the case for distinct observable source states
