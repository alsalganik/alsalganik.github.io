The rise and fall of 1A 1118−61: First evidence for frequency hysteresis of quasi-periodic oscillations in an X-ray pulsar

This paper reports the first evidence for frequency hysteresis of quasi-periodic oscillations in an X-ray pulsar: at the same luminosity, the QPO frequency in 1A 1118−61 is about 35% higher on the outburst rise than on the decline.

Citation

Alexander Salganik, S. S. Tsygankov, S. V. Molkov, H. Xiao, L. Kong, Q. C. Zhao, L. Ji, A. A. Mushtukov, I. Yu. Lapshov, A. A. Lutovinov, A. Yu. Tkachenko, H. Feng, S. N. Zhang, X. B. Li, S. Zhang, J. Poutanen

Submitted to A&A (2026).

View arXiv record

Summary

This paper reports the first evidence for frequency hysteresis of quasi-periodic oscillations in an X-ray pulsar: at the same luminosity, the QPO frequency in 1A 1118−61 is about 35% higher on the outburst rise than on the decline.

  • New phenomenon: QPO frequency hysteresis in an accreting X-ray pulsar
  • The QPO timescale depends on accretion history, not only on luminosity
