RX J0440.9+4431: another supercritical X-ray pulsar

This paper studies RX J0440.9+4431 as a supercritical X-ray pulsar and describes the source's transition into a high-accretion regime.

Summary

This paper studies RX J0440.9+4431 as a supercritical X-ray pulsar and describes the source's transition into a high-accretion regime.

  • Builds the supercritical-accretion case for RX J0440.9+4431
