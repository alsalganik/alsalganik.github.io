First characterization of a new high-mass X-ray binary in LMC eRASSU J050810.4−660653 with SRG/ART-XC, NuSTAR, and Swift

This paper presents a first characterization of the new high-mass X-ray binary eRASSU J050810.4−660653 using data from SRG/ART-XC, NuSTAR, and Swift.

Citation

Alexander Salganik, S. S. Tsygankov, A. A. Lutovinov, A. A. Mushtukov, I. A. Mereminskiy, S. V. Molkov, A. N. Semena

Summary

This paper presents a first characterization of the new high-mass X-ray binary eRASSU J050810.4−660653 using data from SRG/ART-XC, NuSTAR, and Swift.

  • Introduces a newly characterized high-mass X-ray binary
