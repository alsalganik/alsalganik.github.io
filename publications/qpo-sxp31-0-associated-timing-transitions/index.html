Discovery of a 0.8-mHz quasi-periodic oscillation in the transient X-ray pulsar SXP31.0 and associated timing transitions

This paper reports a 0.8-mHz quasi-periodic oscillation in the transient pulsar SXP31.0 and describes the timing transitions associated with that variability.

Citation

Alexander Salganik, S. S. Tsygankov, S. V. Molkov, Igor Yu. Lapshov, A. A. Lutovinov, A. Yu. Tkachenko, A. A. Mushtukov, J. Poutanen

Astronomy & Astrophysics 705, A141 (2026).

View DOI record

Summary

This paper reports a 0.8-mHz quasi-periodic oscillation in the transient pulsar SXP31.0 and describes the timing transitions associated with that variability.

  • Identifies a low-frequency quasi-periodic oscillation in SXP31.0
  • Connects the oscillation to observable timing transitions
