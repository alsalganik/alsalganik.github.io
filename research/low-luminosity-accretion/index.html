Low-Luminosity Accretion

Accretion at low mass-accretion rates, low-luminosity states, and the two-hump X-ray spectra of magnetized neutron stars.

At low mass-accretion rates the X-ray spectra of accreting pulsars depart from the canonical cutoff power law and develop a distinct high-energy hump. This topic collects work on new low-luminosity accretion states and on tracing how the spectrum changes as sources fade.

Focus on this site

  • New stable low-luminosity accretion states
  • Emergence of the high-energy spectral hump as sources fade
  • Transitions to quiescence in Be/X-ray pulsars
